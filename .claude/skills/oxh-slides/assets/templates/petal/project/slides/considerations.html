Progress against goals

A bar on a track with the value in words

Not rings or gauges: a length on one scale is read faster and more accurately (Few, bullet graph spec)

Plans without dates

Now, next, later, four items a column at most

Commits to order without promising dates nobody can keep (house rule)

Dates that matter

A timeline, evenly spaced, seven events at most

Even spacing unless the durations themselves matter (FT Visual Vocabulary)

Status

A word in a pill: on track, waiting, done

Colour never says it alone (WCAG 1.4.1)

Who is on what

Each person’s own flower, their focus in a line

People are recognised by their mark across the team’s tools and the deck (house rule)

A shout-out

One sentence in the person’s own words

Verbatim, with who said it and where; no decorative quote marks (house rule)
