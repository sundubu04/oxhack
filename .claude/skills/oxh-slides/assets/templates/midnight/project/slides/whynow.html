Why now

Sensors got cheap

Before: a soil probe cost $600 and a site visit.

Now: a probe and its radio cost $40 and last five seasons.

Forecasts got local

Before: one rain forecast for a whole county.

Now: an hourly forecast for each field, free, from public models.

What didn’t change

Growers still own the water and the risk

1 in 3

farms in our study faced a watering restriction in 2026

$400

for one agronomist visit, after the damage is done

0

valves open without the grower’s approval

Source: Loamly field study, 2026 season, n = 48 farms (sample data)
