Watering by the calendar

Most farms water on a schedule, and the schedule never knows it rained.

The grower

Guesses the need

By the calendar or by feel, one field at a time

The timer

Runs the schedule

The same minutes every day, rain or shine

The field

Gets what it gets

Too much in the wet rows, too little in the dry ones

By harvest the yield shows it, and next season starts from the same guess.
