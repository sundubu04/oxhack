What we heard, and what we propose in answer

What we heard

Fields are watered by the calendar or by feel, and 38% of runs fall on wet soil.¹

Each farm has its own soil and crops, so one schedule rarely suits them all.²

Growers want control: no valve should open on a field they have not checked.

What we propose

A sensor in each field reads the soil every 15 minutes, all season long.

Each field gets its own plan by 6 a.m., worked out from its soil and its crop.

The grower approves the plan on their phone, and only then do the valves open.

¹ Loamly field study, 2026 season, n = 48 farms. ² Soil and crop mix per farm, as described by your team.
