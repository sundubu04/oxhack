Three things we propose

In short

1

Every field gets a watering plan by 6 a.m.

A sensor reads the soil every 15 minutes, and the plan says when and how much to water that day.

2

Your growers approve every watering

Each plan reaches the grower’s phone with the soil readings behind it, and the valves open only once they approve.

3

We report to the board after every phase

Four phases over six months, each with a field review and a decision on whether to continue.
