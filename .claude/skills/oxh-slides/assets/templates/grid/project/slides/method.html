How we counted

Sample

48 farms growing four crops, 11 to 13 each, from 2 to 140 hectares, in three regions.

Period

Every watering logged from 6 April to 27 September 2026, one full season.

What counts

A watering is one run of the valves on one field; soil is wet above field capacity.

Limits

One season, with a wet August; one probe per field may miss dry patches.
