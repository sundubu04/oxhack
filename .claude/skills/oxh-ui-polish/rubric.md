# UI polish rubric

Twelve lines, each scored 0, 1 or 2 from screenshots alone. 2 matches the "2" example in kind, 0 matches the "0" example, 1 is anything between. When unsure, score the lower number and say why. Decoration never earns a point: a gradient, a shadow or an extra colour that adds no information scores as noise.

The job sentence comes with every critique: who opens this screen, to decide or do what, in how many seconds. Lines 1 and 12 are scored against it.

Pick reference screens in a populated state, with no popover, promo banner or onboarding open. A demo account's near-empty list loses craft points for reasons that have nothing to do with its design.

## Scoring

- Total out of 24 (out of 22 when line 11 is `n/a`). Lines 1 and 12 are the **job** lines, line 8 is **states**, and lines 2 to 7, 9 and 10 are **craft** (16 points).
- References are scored on craft only. They do a neighbouring job on demo data, so the job lines and line 8 say nothing about them.
- **Pass:** no line at 0; lines 1 and 12 at 2; line 8 at 1 or more, from state screenshots; and craft at least the best reference's craft in the same **blind** critique. A critic who knows which screens are references grades toward them, and the same screen moves about 3 points between comparisons with different neighbours, so the bar and the candidate are always scored together, blind.
- The bar is relative because of the first calibration (22 Oct 2026: a dev lab page against Linear, Graphite and GitHub, three blind samples). No screen reached 75% of the total, the references included (11 to 13 of 22). On craft, the references scored 10 to 11 of 16, the one-shot 6, and the page after one polish 10. An absolute bar would never pass, so the loop would never stop early.
- The kit's checked rules (light only, type scale, tokens, contrast, overflow) are not on this rubric: `oxh-ui-kit`'s `check.mjs` checks them before any critique, and a screen that fails them is not scored.
