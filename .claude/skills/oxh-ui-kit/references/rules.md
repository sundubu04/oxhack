# How a screen looks

These rules come from Darling's UI kit (itself drawn from Paper's rules for agent-made interfaces and impeccable's list of what makes an interface look generated), and were measured against the kit itself by design reviewers and by impeccable's detector. `scripts/check.mjs` measures the ones a number can catch (marked **checked**). A repository's own rules come after this file and win where they disagree.

## The rules

1. **One question per card or screen, answered at a glance.** Lead with the answer (a `Stat` or the first row), then the detail. A card's frame already shows its title: never repeat it inside.
2. **Type from the scale only:** 11, 12, 13, 14 (interface), 15 (prose), 17, 21, and 28 for one hero number. 11 is the floor. Four sizes in a card at most (**checked**). Weights 400, 500 and 600, nothing heavier. Numbers that change use tabular figures (`dl-num`; `Stat`, `KV` and `Timeline` do it). Monospace is for ids and code only, not for times or counts.
3. **Light only, colour from tokens only.** No dark backgrounds except the two small ink layers, the toast and the tooltip; no `prefers-color-scheme` styles (**checked**). `--dl-text`, `--dl-text-2` and `--dl-text-3` for text; `--dl-ok`, `--dl-warn`, `--dl-stop` and `--dl-info` for state, always with a word. No gradients of colour, no glows, no text shadows (**checked**); a hard stop that draws a shape, as in the half status mark, is not a gradient. Tint with the opaque washes (`--dl-ok-wash`), which read the same on any row.
4. **Contrast at least 4.5:1** for text under 24px (or under 18.7px bold) and 3:1 above, in every state, busy and hover included (**checked** at rest, against what the text actually sits on). Edges that mark a control, and the marks that show its state, reach 3:1 against what is around them.
5. **Rows: one grammar.** Title on the first line, the reason on the second, one line each with an ellipsis and the full text in `title`. A hairline between rows only, never a box round each; 16px of space separates sections. To open something from a row, the title holds a link or button with `dl-row__link`: a row is never a clickable `<li>`.
6. **Nothing is cut off or spills sideways** (**checked**), at 1440 and at 400 wide. The app frame reflows below 720px: a top bar takes the rail's place and opens it as a drawer (`AppFrame`), and a row's menu gives way to the row's own page.
7. **Empty, loading and error states are designed:** say what is missing in one line and what will fix it. A list that is loading shows still skeleton rows; a failure is a banner or a field error in a sentence, with an icon so it isn't red alone.
8. **Targets at least 24px** for anything you can press (**checked** as a note), and 44px on a touch screen, which the sheet does by itself under `pointer: coarse`.
9. **Motion: only the sheet's three.** `var(--dl-tint)` for colour, `var(--dl-enter)` for something appearing, `var(--dl-travel)` for movement, on `transform`, `opacity` and colours. Never `transition: all`, never width, height, padding or margin, no bounce or overshoot (**checked**). Nothing loops except a spinner while something is actually running.
10. **Words:** sentence case, units on every number ("12 min", not "12"), the person's own words verbatim in quotes, no emoji (use `Icon`; **checked** as a note).

## What makes an interface look generated

Each of these is a choice nobody made, and people spot them in a second.

11. **No eyebrow labels.** No small capitals above a title, a section or a date: "Tomorrow", not "TOMORROW". Name a group with `Head` in sentence case at the normal spacing; a group's label may be smaller than the body if it is heavier (12 at 600). Capitals only in acronyms such as PR and CI (**checked**).
12. **One surface.** A card is the only box on the page: no card inside it, no bordered or shadowed panel round a group; group with space and hairlines (**checked**). A box on the page gets one edge, a border or a shadow, never a hairline with a wide soft shadow; a layer that floats over the page (menu, tooltip, toast, dialog) may have both. No coloured stripe down one side of a box, for selection or for importance: selection is the fill and the weight, importance is a `Status` and a word. No icon in a tinted square above or beside a title (all **checked**).
13. **The pink marks a few things:** the main action, the current item, a link, the focus ring, and the number that answers the card. Hover is grey. A switch, checkbox or radio that is on is ink (`--dl-control-on`), so a list of them doesn't turn the screen pink. One `primary` button per card or section (**checked**); the accent in three places at most on a screen (**checked** as a note).
14. **Every control has its states:** at rest, hover, focus (the 2px pink ring), pressed, disabled (the shape kept, the contrast dropped), busy (the label stays and a spinner joins it; no more presses) and failed (the control is back at rest and the reason is said beside it, in words, with an icon). A menu, tooltip or popover opens in a portal and is never cut off by the box it opens from (**checked** as a note).
15. **Rhythm:** spacing from 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32, tight inside a group and wider between groups, never the same gap everywhere; 2 and 6 only inside a control. Control heights 28 and 32 (24 and 28 compact). Text sits at least 8px from the edge of a card, field or button (tags, keys and avatars excepted), and wrapped text has a line height of 1.35 or more (**checked** as notes). A title is 1.15 times its body or more. Corners nest: an inner radius is the outer less the space between.
16. **Words that sound like a person:** no exclamation marks, no "Welcome back", no filler ("Something went wrong", "No data", "Coming soon", "Get started"); say what happened and what to do next. Labels and buttons in sentence case, not Title Case. A button says its verb ("Remove", not "OK"). The ellipsis character (…), not three dots, and on a button only when a question follows. Say each thing once (all **checked** as notes).
17. **Behaviour comes from the platform or from Base UI, not from scratch.** Native inputs for checkboxes, radios and form selects; `overlays.tsx` (Base UI) for menus, value pickers, dialogs, tooltips and toasts, so focus, Escape, arrow keys and screen readers work. Style state on ARIA attributes. A loss that can't be undone is confirmed in a dialog that names it; one that can be undone gets a toast with Undo instead. Single-key shortcuts have a setting that turns them off, and the page starts with a skip link past the rail.
18. **Icons are Lucide, at 12, 16 or 20px, from `assets/icons.json`.** One stroke and one grid for every glyph. An icon never stands for state on its own, never sits in a tinted tile, and nothing means "AI" with sparkles, wands or robots.

## Make it interesting with information, not decoration

Calm is not empty. A screen earns attention by showing the shape of its data: a day as a `Timeline` with the now line, a count with the `Spark` of the last two weeks beside it, progress as a `Meter` with its numbers in words beside it, the next thing as a countdown, rows grouped by what needs the person now. Prefer one strong visual (a timeline, a meter, a big number) over many small labels. Size a card to what it shows: no large empty areas, no scroll for the main view.
