Pink UI kit

Every piece of the kit in every state, drawn by the kit's own stylesheet, inlined. Neutral greys and the system font, one pink for the thing that matters on a screen. The same files are the oxh-ui-kit skill in this repo.

Density
Every piece below is the stylesheet itself. Sample data throughout.

02Colour

Greys do almost all the work. The pink is one hue in twelve steps, built in OKLCH around flow-er's flower; four state colours each come with a word, a shape and a wash. Everything is a --dl-* token, so a screen never writes a colour of its own.

03Type, space, shape and motion

The system font, because a console is a tool people open every day and the platform's own face is the one they read fastest: SF Pro on a Mac, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android. Eight sizes, three weights, one spacing scale, five radii and three durations; anything else is a value the kit didn't choose.

04Symbols

Icons come from Lucide, not from hand-drawn paths: one 24 px grid, a 2 px round stroke, corners and terminals drawn by the same people, and a name for every glyph so two screens never draw "settings" two ways. At 16 px the stroke renders at 1.33 px, the weight of 14 px text. State is never an icon alone: it is a shaped mark and a word.

05Controls, in every state

Rule 14 says every control has its states; here they are, side by side, drawn by the sheet's own rules. The hover, focus and pressed columns are the real :hover, :focus-visible and :active rules copied onto a class by the page's build, so this table cannot drift from the CSS. Move the pointer over the rest column to compare.

06Feedback, waiting and overlays

What a screen says when something happens, while it waits, and what opens on top of it. Each one says what happened and what to do next, in a sentence a person would say.

07Showing data

A console earns attention with the shape of its data, not with decoration: a number that answers the question, its trend beside it, progress as a bar, a day as a line with now on it, and rows that say why they are there.
