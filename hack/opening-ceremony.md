# OxHack 2026 — opening ceremony brief

From the organisers' opening-ceremony deck ("Opening Ceremony Slides", Google Slides `1Cl8yiHaEAHAV7eKix_oufqp9wXy0O_jrjImG92Y8YQw`), read 24 Oct 2026. ## The event

- **OxHack 2026**, Oxford. In person + online. Run by **Hackathons UK** with **MLH** (coach: Oscar). ~350 hackers, 2 days.
- **25 hours of hacking** — the clocks go back on Sun 25 Oct, so there's an extra hour.
- Cup stacking Sat 20:30 BST; demos and "I Demoed" stickers on Sunday.
- **"Best Handmade Project"** prize, with thanks to **Citadel** — a handmade/physical build track.
- Code of conduct: MLH + Hackathons UK. Don't use the venue AV systems. First aid at reception.

## Tracks and challenges

### HRT (Hudson River Trading) — the one to match
**"HRT <> OxHacks Challenge 2026: Best use of Data for Predictions & Decision Making."** HRT describe themselves as "a tech company in finance." Their work is statistical analysis and ML over vast datasets, data pipelines, predictive modelling. No explicit weighting given in the deck.

### Delta — "Built with Delta" (a process/tooling prize)
Delta (delta.dev) is a collaborative coding tool (CRDT-based, with agents). Build with teammates and agents in Delta, from ideation through testing, and share your Delta threads.
- **Judging:** Speed & Performance **30%**, Technical Implementation **25%**, Innovation **25%**, Polish & Usability **20%**.
- **Wants:** projects that feel fast (snappy UX, no friction), solve a real problem ("something you'd actually open tomorrow"), and are thoughtful (clean code, smart architecture).
- **Tokens:** OpenRouter tokens inside Delta. Download delta.dev/download.
