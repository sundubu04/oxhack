# Brief: arm control (Clockwork studio)

- Skeleton: Linear's three columns. Rail 232 px (workspace, find, two pages, samples, recent, service status at the foot); work area; inspector 340 px with a hairline, its own scroll.
- Density: Linear rows 28–32 px, 13 px interface text, one 21 px title; properties as label and value pairs, 8 to 12 per screen.
- Row anatomy: rail items icon, label, count right. Inspector rows label left, value right, one line each.
- Recraft and Replit: the canvas owns the middle and the prompt is one line with its tools inside it; the result appears where you look, not in a list.
- State words: Linear uses a mark plus a word (Backlog, Todo). Ours: Ready, Out of reach, Past servo limits, Drawing, Pen up, Erasing, Not connected.
- Empty: Recraft shows an empty frame on the canvas. Loading: the step that is running. Error: a banner under the prompt.
- References leave out: time and progress (no transport), live readouts, any physical scale.
- Ours: take the three columns, the one-line prompt, the canvas as the hero and the label/value inspector. Add a transport under the canvas (play, start again, erase, scrub, speed), a hero number (time to draw) and live joint meters.
- House rules change: light only, the kit's pink accent instead of Linear's indigo, the system font instead of Inter, compact density, no cards (the inspector is a column, not panels), Lucide icons only.
