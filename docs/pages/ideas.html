OxHack idea board

What to build this weekend. Most of the strong ideas share one shape — a phone-vision game that collects a time-stamped dataset and turns it into a prediction — which lines up the main theme and the best sponsor prizes at once. One route is different: a hardware clock that draws and erases itself.

24 Oct 2026Theme: TimeScience-fair demo, 4–5 min

The one shape that wins across tracks

point the camera→time-stamped data→a prediction→a decision / the next move

The vision is Gemini (reads the world) or Presage (reads your pulse). The data grows over the event, so it is a time-series — the native fit for Snowflake / Tiger Data. The prediction and decision are HRT's brief. And because the data is time-stamped and things change over time, it is the Time theme. One build, many prizes.

Top picks

N1

Chronocache

real-life Easter eggs · Gemini

Geocaching where the camera is the lock and time is the gate. Hide an egg pinned to a real view; set it to open now, at a future date (a capsule), only in a window, or on a schedule. Every hide and find trains a "where and when to look next" forecast. Demo: hide one on the table with a 2-minute unlock, a judge finds it live.

G1 / G2

SpawnCast / PriceDex

catch-and-collect · Gemini

Pokémon-Go-style: photograph real things into a dex; the time-stamped sightings train a model that predicts the next spawns, which players chase. PriceDex is the HRT-sharp twin — snap price tags, build crowd-sourced inflation data with a price forecast ("changes in value over time" is the organisers' own example).

N2

Time Capsule

real-life Easter eggs · Gemini + ElevenLabs

The warmer, simpler cut: pin a message, photo or voice note to a real spot that stays locked until a future time. "Leave something for whoever stands here in six months." Pure Time, and genuinely moving — an easy, memorable demo.

G4

Calm Catch

biometric · Presage

The biggest instant wow at a table: a creature you catch only by lowering your own heart rate, read from your face by Presage. Judges play it with their pulse. Thinner dataset and a slower 20-second reading, but unforgettable.

H9 / H3

Camera drawing clock

hardware · plotter / Fourier arm · Gemini

The hardware route, with a camera. A machine that draws and erases like Maarten Baas's Real Time clock, but it draws what the camera sees — a portrait of whoever's there, a new face each minute, the erased faces building a mural of the event. Or it plots a live forecast (H3), and the camera watches the surface to correct its own lines. A natural Best Handmade + Hardware Lab entry, plus Gemini.

Route D — hardware: a drawing clock, with a camera

A machine that draws, erases and redraws — a robotic homage to Maarten Baas's Real Time clock, via a plotter, a polargraph, or a Fourier/epicycle arm. The camera is the fun part: it can draw what it sees (a portrait, the room), read a real clock and restyle it, track your finger in the air, or watch its own surface to correct the line. Targets Best Handmade (Citadel) and the Hardware Lab; the camera adds Gemini (or Presage), and the data spin adds Time + HRT.

Decide early

Pick a lane: eggs (A), catch-game (B), or the hardware clock (D).A and B share one engine (vision → spatiotemporal data → forecast), so that choice is mostly flavour. D is a different bet — a physical build with its own risk (erasing) and its own prizes (Handmade, Hardware Lab), and a camera keeps it in the vision family. PHP (C) is the fallback.
Gemini or Presage.Gemini (web app, catch the world) is more buildable and the data is richer. Presage (native SDK, read the body) is the biggest table wow but slower and more friction. Hybrids exist but cost build time.
Build in Delta?It's a stackable prize and rewards snappy UX, but check it coexists with the repo's Claude Code setup before committing the night to it.
Confirm the clock.The event times in the repo are estimates from "25 hours, demos Sunday" — get the real submission and judging times from Discord.
Scoring & where the detail lives
  • Scores are our own filters out of 25 (fun to demo, data value, prize fit, buildable in ~20 h, works in a 4–5 min table demo). They sort; they don't decide.
  • Every idea, with the full sub-scores and the long list, is in hack/ideas.md in the repo.
  • The tracks and prizes are in hack/event.md and hack/opening-ceremony.md; the PHP research is its own page, linked above.
  • Presage measures heart rate and breathing from a face in ~20 s (FDA-cleared, free for the event); Gemini is general multimodal vision.
